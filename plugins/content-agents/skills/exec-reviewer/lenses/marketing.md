# Lens: marketing (final reviewer for marketing emails, web copy, paid campaigns, content pieces)

Status: placeholder. This lens is drafted from shared guides and will be replaced with the reviewer's own instructions and seed reviews.

## What this reviewer weighs most
- **LMK-001** On-message: the piece lands the one message agreed at intake, aligned with the messaging guide.
- **LMK-002** Positioning: the three benefits (accurate, under control, for less) are used correctly, one leading.
- **LMK-003** Alignment before writing: if the piece contradicts the foundational messaging, that's a blocker, not a line edit.
- **LMK-004** Brand voice: expert, clear, human.

## Typical questions
- What's the one thing we want the reader to believe after this?
- Which persona is this for, and does the opening speak their language?
- Is every number exactly as published?
