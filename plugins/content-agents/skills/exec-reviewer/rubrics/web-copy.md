# Rubric: web copy, paid campaigns, paid ads

## Must-haves
- **WEB-001** Jargon budget by page depth: top-level pages build trust with minimal jargon; mid-level pages explain why guarantees hold; deep pages explain how.
- **WEB-002** System before outcome; never a benefit-only headline without a mechanism nearby.
- **WEB-003** You-phrasing applies most strictly here (VOI-005).
- **WEB-004** Tight sentences; structure carries the argument.
- **WEB-005** Capability names exactly as in MSG-003; flag "Model Gateway".
- **WEB-006** One primary CTA per section; CTAs short once the copy has done the work.

## Paid ads extras
- **WEB-101** One message, one proof point, one CTA; numerals.
- **WEB-102** Headline works without the body.
- **WEB-103** No claim the landing page doesn't back up.
