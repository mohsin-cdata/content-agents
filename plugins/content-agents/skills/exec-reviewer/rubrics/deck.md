# Rubric: deck

## Must-haves
- **DCK-001** Slide titles are verb-led statements of the point, not topic labels ("Agents inherit each user's permissions", not "Security").
- **DCK-002** One idea per slide; a reader skimming titles alone gets the whole argument.
- **DCK-003** Opens in the audience's problem; product appears after the problem is established.
- **DCK-004** Mechanism slides (how it works) come before outcome slides (what you get).
- **DCK-005** Stats quoted exactly with what was measured (MSG-006, MSG-007).
- **DCK-006** Sentence case below the deck title; no walls of text; numerals in charts.
- **DCK-007** Clear final ask or next step.
