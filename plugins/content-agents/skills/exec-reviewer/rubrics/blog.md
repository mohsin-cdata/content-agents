# Rubric: blog

## Must-haves
- **BLG-001** One unheaded intro (one or two paragraphs) before the first header, opening in the reader's problem.
- **BLG-002** Headline in Title Case; every other header in sentence case, no end punctuation.
- **BLG-003** Each section advances the argument; no section restates an earlier one.
- **BLG-004** Mechanism before outcome in every section that claims a benefit.
- **BLG-005** One CTA section at the end: brief summary, product name with link, closing header in active voice. No "Conclusion" header. No exclamation points.
- **BLG-006** Jargon budget matches depth: technical blogs stay peer-to-peer and detailed; thought-leadership blogs keep jargon low.
- **BLG-007** Claims and stats have a source or come from `messaging.md` exactly.

## Common exec stops
- Intro warms up instead of earning the second sentence.
- Product pitch arrives before the reader understands the problem.
- Ends with a recap instead of the strongest claim.
