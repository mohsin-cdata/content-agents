# Rubric: email (sales and marketing)

## Must-haves (both)
- **EML-001** Subject line 40 to 50 characters or fewer; specific and benefit-forward, not clever.
- **EML-002** Preheader extends the subject; never repeats it.
- **EML-003** Three to five short paragraphs maximum; one point per email.
- **EML-004** Exactly one clear CTA.
- **EML-005** Numerals for all numbers.
- **EML-006** Contractions and direct address; formal emails get ignored.

## Sales email extras
- **EML-101** First line is about the recipient's world, not CData. No product name in the first sentence.
- **EML-102** One proof point, matched to the persona (MSG-009), quoted exactly.
- **EML-103** CTA is a low-friction next step (a question or a short call), not "buy now".

## Marketing email extras
- **EML-201** Matches the campaign's single message; no second offer competing with the CTA.
- **EML-202** Consistent with the landing page it links to (same claim, same numbers).
