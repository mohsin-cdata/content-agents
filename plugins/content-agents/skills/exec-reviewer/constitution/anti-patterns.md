# Anti-patterns

Recurring mistakes execs keep flagging. One dated entry each. Add new ones through feedback (`/content-agents:feedback`), not by editing locally.

| ID | Added | Pattern | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| ANT-001 | 2026-11-18 | Leading with "AI gateway" or the product name in the first line | Open in the reader's problem (PRI-001) |
| ANT-002 | 2026-11-18 | Blending the three benchmark stats (e.g. "97% fewer tokens", "178 times more accurate") | Quote each stat exactly (MSG-006) |
| ANT-003 | 2026-11-18 | Feature lists with no mechanism ("fast, secure, and scalable") | Explain one mechanism, then the outcome (PRI-002) |
| ANT-004 | 2026-11-18 | "300+ connectors" or any specific count | "Hundreds of data sources" (MSG-004) |
| ANT-005 | 2026-11-18 | Closing with a recap paragraph or "Conclusion" header | End on the strongest specific claim; one CTA section (PRI-008) |

Seed status: these are drafted from the brand voice and messaging guides. Replace or confirm them once real exec reviews are added.
